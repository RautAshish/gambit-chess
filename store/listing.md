## Full description
Gambit Chess is a fast, elegant chess app that respects you: no ads, no
account, no tracking — it even plays entirely offline.

FEATURES
• Four difficulty levels powered by a built-in engine, or switch on the
  legendary Stockfish 18 for a real challenge
• 137 tactics puzzles, every single one machine-verified — mate in 1 and
  mate in 2, with hints and saved progress
• Pass & Play on one phone, with chess clocks (Bullet to Rapid, Fischer
  increments)
• Online play with friends by room code — runs on your own free Firebase,
  so your games belong to you
• Full rules: castling, en passant, promotion, threefold repetition,
  fifty-move rule, insufficient material
• Undo/redo, saved games, PGN move list, board themes, legal-move hints,
  sounds and haptics
• Dark, distraction-free design

PRIVACY
No ads. No analytics. No data collection. See our privacy policy.
