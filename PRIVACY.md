# Emersion Chess — Privacy Policy

Emersion Chess is built to work offline and to collect nothing.

- No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party SDKs phoning home.
- Games, puzzles progress, and settings are stored only on your device.
- Optional online play connects exclusively to a Firebase project THAT YOU
  create and control. In that mode, an anonymous identifier and the chess
  moves of your games are stored in YOUR Firestore database, visible only to
  the two participants of each game. We (the app developers) operate no
  server and receive no data whatsoever.
- The app requests the INTERNET permission solely for the optional online
  mode and the VIBRATE permission for move haptics.
- No data is sold or shared. Deleting the app deletes all local data;
  deleting your Firebase project deletes all online data.

Contact: via the source repository's issue tracker.
